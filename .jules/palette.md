## 2024-03-08 - Enhance Button Accessibility in TaskEditor
**Learning:** Adding `aria-label` to buttons without text or with ambiguous text (like "Auto-Enhance with Gemini AI" without focus states, or toggle buttons) significantly improves the experience for screen reader users. Adding `focus:outline-none focus:ring-2 focus:ring-.../50` provides crucial visual feedback for keyboard navigation. For toggle buttons using `aria-pressed`, the `aria-label` should be a static description of the feature (e.g., "Toggle collaboration sharing") rather than changing dynamically to avoid confusing double-announcements.
**Action:** Always verify that interactive elements, especially icon-only buttons or custom toggles, have clear `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` attributes and proper visible focus states. Ensure `aria-pressed` toggles use static labels.
